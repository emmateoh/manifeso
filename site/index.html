What we install

Three trades, one team on site. Main contractors give us the low-current package and we take it from first fix to handover.

CCTV installation

IP cameras on walls, poles and ceilings, cabled back to the recorder and set up so the monitoring room sees every angle it needs.

  • Bullet, dome and PTZ cameras
  • Wall, pole and ceiling mounting
  • Recorder (NVR) setup
  • Monitoring screens

Structured cabling

Copper data cabling from the rack to every outlet: pulled through the containment, dressed into the rack, terminated and labelled.

  • Cat6 and Cat6A cabling
  • Racks and patch panels
  • Cable tray and containment
  • Cable and port labelling

Fiber splicing

Fiber backbone between floors and buildings, spliced and terminated in the enclosure, ready for the network to come up.

  • Fusion splicing
  • Splice trays and enclosures
  • Fiber patch panels
  • Backbone between floors
