About us

Manifeso Trading Contracting & Services is a technical installation company based in Doha, Qatar.

Who we are

We are the people who do the technical work on site. Main contractors, developers and building owners bring us in for the low-current systems: CCTV, structured cabling, fiber, and the control panels that sit behind them.

Our team installs the cameras, pulls and terminates the cable, splices the fiber and labels everything so the next person can find it. We hand over systems that are finished, tidy and working.

Kakana Contracting and Services Company

Kakana Contracting and Services Company is a subsidiary of Manifeso Trading.

Why contractors call us

We do the installation ourselves rather than passing it on, so the people you meet are the people on your site. We keep the work neat because the racks and panels are what the client sees at handover.
