## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS with a little vanilla JS, no build step (user's choice). Deployed by GitHub Pages from the `emmateoh/manifeso` repo; custom domain `manifesotrading.com` (registered on Cloudflare). Contact form will be Formspree; the user supplies the endpoint later.

## Users

Primary: main contractors and their project/MEP managers in Qatar who subcontract the low-current (ELV) package on towers and large buildings — they need a team that turns up, installs to spec, tests, labels and hands over without chasing. Secondary: building owners, developers and facility managers hiring directly.

## Product Purpose

Company website for Manifeso Trading Contracting & Services (Doha, Qatar): the technical team that installs CCTV, structured cabling and fiber splicing. Its job is to show finished work so a contractor trusts the team with the next package and makes contact (WhatsApp, call, email, contact form).

## Positioning

The hands-on installation team itself, not a reseller: the evidence is the team's own site photos from named towers in Lusail and Doha — dressed racks, labelled cables, terminated panels, cameras on the wall.

## Operating Context

Visitors are often on a phone on site or forwarded a link on WhatsApp. They judge by workmanship photos (cable dressing, labelling, test results) and named projects. Bilingual market (English primary; Arabic appears in the current site).
